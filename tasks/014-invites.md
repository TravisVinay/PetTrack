## Goal
Share the household with roommates, family or a sitter.

## Steps
- Owner creates an invite link/code (expires; single- or multi-use)
- Joining via the link adds the user as a member
- Members list in Settings; owner can remove members

## Done when
- A second account joins via link and sees the same pets and logs
